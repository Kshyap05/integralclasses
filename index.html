Recommended Pedagogy

Area Model of Binomial Expansion

Bridge the gap between arithmetic multiplication arrays and algebraic identities $(x+a)(x+b)$ by letting students visually dissect 2D area tiles before introducing formulaic FOIL.

Frequent Student Pitfall

Students write (x + 3)² = x² + 9, omitting middle terms because they distribute the exponent rather than computing the composite rectangular area.

Low-Floor Inquiry Hook Question

"If a garden square is lengthened by 3 meters on each side, why doesn't its total area just increase by 9?"
